How to Play Sudoku

Core Grid Rules
  • Numbers range from 1 to 9
  • Each row must contain all numbers from 1 to 9 (no repetitions)
  • Each column must contain all numbers from 1 to 9 (no repetitions)
  • Each 3x3 box must contain all numbers from 1 to 9 (no repetitions)
Scoring System
  • Correct placement of each number +2 pts
  • Incorrect placement of each number -5 pts
Bonus Points
  • Completion of full row +4 pts
  • Completion of full column +4 pts
  • Completion of 3x3 Box +6 pts
  • Completion of a Tier (3 horizontal Boxes) +8 pts
  • Completion of a Tower (3 vertical Boxes) +8 pts
  • Completion of the entire Grid (Victory) +10 pts
